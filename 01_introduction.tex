\section{Background and Motivation}
\label{sec:background}
\label{sec:hexapole_overview}
\label{sec:prior_work}

\subsection*{English Draft}

Precise force application and measurement at the piconewton-to-nanonewton scale are essential for modern biomechanics and cell mechanics studies. Common platforms include optical tweezers \cite{ashkin1986observation}, atomic force microscopy (AFM) \cite{binnig1986afm}, and magnetic tweezers \cite{neuman2008singlemolecule}. Among them, magnetic actuation is attractive because it provides contactless force generation without photothermal loading and allows decoupled force-position sensing in aqueous environments \cite{gosse2002magnetic, devlaminck2012magnetic}.

The hexapole electromagnetic actuator developed by the Menq group provides six independently driven poles arranged in a symmetric geometry, enabling 3-D magnetic force generation with over-actuated redundancy \cite{zhang2011design}. This platform has evolved from current-based force modeling to Hall-sensor-based flux modeling and high-bandwidth control, culminating in ultra-precise untethered manipulation \cite{meng2023ultraprecise} and piconewton-scale interaction-force control \cite{meng2024piconewton}.

In this thesis, the control architecture is treated as a three-layer chain:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{System Identification}: reliable 6\(\times\)6 dynamic modeling from frequency-response data.
  \item \textbf{Flux Control}: high-bandwidth inner-loop control under hysteresis, cross-coupling, and ringing-zero constraints.
  \item \textbf{Force Generation}: mapping desired 3-D force to six-channel flux-voltage commands with quantifiable output accuracy.
\end{enumerate}

The prior baseline is clear: (i) hexapole design and current-based force model \cite{zhang2011design}; (ii) optimal current allocation for over-actuated force production \cite{long2021optimal}; (iii) Hall-sensor-based force model and inverse model \cite{long2022hallsensor}; (iv) high-speed untethered manipulation with layered control \cite{meng2023ultraprecise}; and (v) high-bandwidth flux control with piconewton interaction-force regulation \cite{meng2024piconewton}. However, the literature mainly reports final performance, while layer-specific design choices and their quantitative impact are less systematically compared.

Specifically, three technical gaps motivate this thesis:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Identification gap}: limited systematic discussion on fitting strategy sensitivity, model quality metrics, and cross-configuration consistency in 6\(\times\)6 identification.
  \item \textbf{Flux-control gap}: limited component-level comparison between PI and model-based control, especially for disturbance observer, feedback law, and prefilter roles.
  \item \textbf{Force-generation gap}: limited quantitative analysis of how command scheduling and nonlinear transfer affect force-accuracy and bandwidth bottlenecks.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{中文對照草稿}

在生物力學與細胞力學研究中，皮牛頓到奈牛頓等級的精確施力與量測是關鍵能力。主流平台包含 optical tweezers \cite{ashkin1986observation}、AFM \cite{binnig1986afm} 與 magnetic tweezers \cite{neuman2008singlemolecule}。其中，磁性致動在水溶液中具有非接觸、低熱負載與力位移解耦等優勢 \cite{gosse2002magnetic, devlaminck2012magnetic}。

Menq 團隊提出的 hexapole electromagnetic actuator 以六個對稱磁極形成 over-actuated 架構，可進行三維力輸出 \cite{zhang2011design}。該平台從 current-based 力模型逐步發展至 Hall-sensor-based flux modeling 與高頻寬控制，最終達成 ultra-precise untethered manipulation \cite{meng2023ultraprecise} 與 piconewton interaction-force control \cite{meng2024piconewton}。

本論文將控制鏈明確定義為三層：System Identification、Flux Control、Force Generation。核心目標不是僅展示最終效能，而是量化各層設計決策對整體性能的影響，建立可比較、可重現的分析框架。

前人已建立完整技術基線，但文獻多聚焦最終結果，對於「各層方法為何有效」與「不同方法差異來源」的量化比較相對不足。本論文對應的三個缺口為：6\(\times\)6 鑑別方法的敏感度與一致性分析、PI 與 model-based flux control 的組件級比較、以及 force generation 中 command scheduling 與非線性傳遞對精度與頻寬的影響。

\section{Research Objective and Scope}
\label{sec:objectives}

\subsection*{English Draft}

The primary objective of this thesis is to establish a decision-complete control-analysis framework for a Hall-sensor-based hexapole actuator, with quantitative traceability from identification to force output.

The thesis addresses the following research questions:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{RQ1 (Identification)}: How can a reliable 6\(\times\)6 model be identified and validated across different experimental configurations?
  \item \textbf{RQ2 (Flux Control)}: How do PI and model-based controllers differ at the component level, and which physical issues are addressed by each control component?
  \item \textbf{RQ3 (Force Generation)}: How do command-scheduling choices and nonlinear transfer characteristics propagate into force-accuracy and bandwidth limits?
\end{enumerate}

To answer these questions, this thesis provides four contributions:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item A reproducible 6\(\times\)6 identification workflow with quality checks and cross-configuration comparison.
  \item A component-level analysis of model-based flux control (disturbance observer, feedback law, prefilter) with PI baseline comparison.
  \item A force-generation analysis that quantifies scheduling-induced distortion and bandwidth bottlenecks.
  \item FPGA implementation and experiment-oriented verification for the inner-loop and force-generation chain.
\end{enumerate}

This thesis is intentionally scoped to the control chain from identification to force generation. Outer-loop motion-control extensions are discussed as downstream context rather than the primary contribution target of this chapter.

\subsection*{中文對照草稿}

本論文的主要目標是建立一套 Hall-sensor-based hexapole 平台的控制分析框架，使結果可從 identification 一路追溯到 force output，且具備量化可驗證性。

本論文聚焦三個研究問題：
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{RQ1（Identification）}：如何在不同實驗配置下建立並驗證可靠的 6\(\times\)6 模型？
  \item \textbf{RQ2（Flux Control）}：PI 與 model-based 控制器在組件層級的差異是什麼？各組件分別解決哪些物理問題？
  \item \textbf{RQ3（Force Generation）}：command scheduling 與非線性傳遞如何造成 force-accuracy 與 bandwidth 的限制？
\end{enumerate}

對應上述問題，本論文提出四項貢獻：可重現的 6\(\times\)6 鑑別流程、PI 與 model-based 的組件級比較、force generation 誤差與瓶頸分析、以及 FPGA 實作驗證。章節範圍刻意聚焦在 ID 到 force generation 的主鏈；outer-loop motion control 於後續章節作延伸討論。

\section{Dissertation Overview}
\label{sec:outline}

\subsection*{English Draft}

The dissertation is organized to directly map chapters to the research questions:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Chapter 2} establishes the modeling foundations required for identification and control analysis.
  \item \textbf{Chapter 3} addresses RQ1 through the 6\(\times\)6 identification workflow and model-quality evaluation.
  \item \textbf{Chapter 4} addresses RQ2 through PI vs model-based flux-control analysis at both system and component levels.
  \item \textbf{Chapter 5} addresses RQ3 through force-generation scheduling analysis and bandwidth bottleneck decomposition.
  \item \textbf{Chapter 6} presents implementation details and experiment-aligned verification of the control chain.
  \item \textbf{Chapter 7} discusses motion-control simulation and downstream implications.
  \item \textbf{Chapter 8} summarizes validated findings and outlines future work.
\end{enumerate}

This chapter-level mapping is designed so that each major claim in Chapter 1 has a corresponding quantitative verification path in later chapters.

\subsection*{中文對照草稿}

本論文的章節安排直接對應研究問題：Chapter 2 建立建模基礎；Chapter 3 回答 RQ1（6\(\times\)6 鑑別與模型品質）；Chapter 4 回答 RQ2（PI 與 model-based flux control 比較）；Chapter 5 回答 RQ3（force generation 排程與頻寬瓶頸）；Chapter 6 提供 FPGA 與實驗驗證；Chapter 7 討論運動控制延伸；Chapter 8 總結與展望。

因此，Chapter 1 的每一項主張都對應到後續章節的量化驗證路徑，避免只停留在概念敘述。
