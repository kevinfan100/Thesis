\begin{abstractzh}
\setcounter{page}{1}

六極電磁致動器透過六個對稱配置的磁極尖端，在三維空間中對水溶液中的磁性微探針施加磁梯度力，已成功實現超精密高速無繫繩操控與皮牛頓等級的力量控制。系統控制採三層架構——內迴圈磁通控制、力生成控制與外迴圈運動控制——各層面對磁滯效應、通道耦合、非最小相位零點、過驅動分配、以及非線性力模型等不同物理挑戰。前人研究已系統性地解決上述技術問題並達成優異實驗成果，但對各層控制設計中的設計選擇與量化影響分析相對有限。

本論文針對系統鑑別、磁通控制與力生成三個層級，進行逐層深入的設計分析與量化比較。在系統鑑別層，建立 $6\times6$ 多輸入多輸出系統的完整鑑別方法，在 11 組實驗配置下分析擬合策略對模型精度的影響。在磁通控制層，提出 R-Controller 觀測器型控制律，以統一推導框架建立，並對擾動觀測器、回饋控制與前饋預濾波三個組件進行個別分析，在組件層級量化比例-積分控制器與模型基方案的效能差異。在力生成層，分析期望磁通電壓排程方法對力精度的影響，並辨識各級頻寬瓶頸。R-Controller 在 FPGA（Arria V）上以 100~kHz 即時實現，實驗量測的頻率響應與理論預測進行對照。

系統性的逐層分析同時衍生出實用的工程效益：自動化鑑別流程、簡化的控制律推導、泛化的參數設計方式、以及參數化模擬工具，使設計流程具備可重現性與可移植性。

\keywordzh{六極電磁致動器、磁通控制、R-Controller、系統鑑別、FPGA}

\end{abstractzh}


\begin{abstracten}
\begin{spacing}{1.5}

The hexapole electromagnetic actuator generates three-dimensional magnetic gradient forces on magnetic microprobes in aqueous solutions through six symmetrically arranged pole tips, enabling ultra-precise high-speed untethered manipulation and piconewton-scale force control. The system employs a three-layer control architecture---inner-loop flux control, force generation control, and outer-loop motion control---where each layer faces distinct physical challenges including hysteresis, channel coupling, non-minimum phase zeros, over-actuated allocation, and nonlinear force models. Prior research has systematically addressed these technical challenges and achieved outstanding experimental performance, yet the analysis of design choices and their quantitative impact within each control layer remains limited.

This thesis conducts a layer-by-layer design analysis with quantitative comparisons across three levels: system identification, flux control, and force generation. At the system identification level, a complete $6\times6$ multi-input multi-output identification method is established, and the influence of fitting strategies on model accuracy is analyzed across 11 experimental configurations. At the flux control level, the R-Controller---an observer-based control law derived within a unified framework---is proposed, with individual analysis of the disturbance observer, feedback controller, and feedforward prefilter components, quantifying the performance difference between proportional-integral controllers and model-based approaches at the component level. At the force generation level, the impact of desired flux voltage scheduling methods on force accuracy is analyzed, and bandwidth bottlenecks at each stage are identified. The R-Controller is implemented in real time at 100~kHz on an FPGA (Arria V), with experimentally measured frequency responses compared against theoretical design predictions.

The systematic layer-by-layer analysis simultaneously yields practical engineering benefits: an automated identification pipeline, a simplified control law derivation, generalized bandwidth-based design parameterization, and a parameterized simulation tool, making the design process reproducible and portable.

\keyworden{Hexapole Electromagnetic Actuator, Magnetic Flux Control, Observer-Based Controller, System Identification, FPGA}

\end{spacing}
\end{abstracten}
